Program overview

Learn how SSA runs — from every angle

Interns spend three weeks in each of five departments. You work with board members, join meetings and projects, and help keep SSA moving.

The goal is simple: understand SSA as a whole, build new skills, and find where you fit best.

How rotations work

Five areas. Three weeks each.

The rotational program builds stronger relationships with the board, develops you across departments, and helps place you long-term.

  • Exposure to each department's responsibilities
  • Participation in relevant meetings
  • Collaboration with existing board members
  • Insight into workflow, planning, and decision-making

By the end, you should have experience with event planning, grants and funding, Somali Night, promo content, outreach, monthly planning, and spotting roadblocks early.

Expectations & culture

Who we're looking for

People who genuinely want to be part of SSA, contribute to the group dynamic, and help keep the community fun, welcoming, and inclusive.

If you have ideas for how we can better support members, this is the place to bring them. We want people who take initiative and are willing to lead — we'll learn from you as much as you learn from us.
