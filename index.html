About

A home for Somali students at the University of Minnesota

SSA is a student-led organization dedicated to promoting Somali culture, supporting Somali students, and building meaningful connections across campus and the broader Twin Cities community.

Culture

Language, food, history, poetry, fashion, storytelling, and cultural education.

Community

Events and partnerships that connect students, alumni, families, and local leaders.

Leadership

Roles in a board that promotes leadership development and opportunities for students to grow and lead

Programs

How SSA programs its events.

Every SSA event falls into one of two buckets. It shapes the size, the goal, and how we plan it.

Campus-Focused

Campus-Focused Events

Designed for broad student reach. Typically larger, more social, and attendance-driven — the goal is visibility, engagement, and campus presence.

  • Higher attendance targets
  • More promotional infrastructure required
  • Energy-forward programming
  • Designed to grow SSA's campus footprint
  • Often tied to seasonal moments or high-traffic windows
Community-Focused

Community-Focused Events

Intentional and specific. They prioritize depth over scale — conversation, reflection, service, and genuine community value. The goal is connection, not just attendance.

  • More thoughtful, discussion-driven programming
  • Designed for targeted audiences or specific purposes
  • Impact-oriented over attendance-oriented
  • Often more intimate in format and setting
  • Reflects SSA's responsibility to the broader Somali community
